No. 01 · Jaargang 1842–2026 · Delft, Zuid-Holland

From a canal-side minor programme to BK City從運河邊的建築學程到設計名校

How architecture in Delft grew from a minor programme for engineers into one of the world's leading schools: a lecture by Berlage, a traditionalist school, a modernist turn, a student vote, a fire, and a red-brick building that became a city. Scroll through twelve stations; the map follows.

台夫特的建築教育，如何從工程師的一門輔系學程，走成世界頂尖的建築學院？貝拉赫的一場演講、傳統主義的學派、現代主義的轉向、學生的一人一票、一場大火，以及一棟被改造成「城市」的紅磚建築。往下捲動十二個站點，地圖會跟著移動。

01
No. 01 · 1842

A minor programme for engineers工程師的輔系學程

King Willem II founds the Royal Academy in Delft to train civil engineers, many of them bound for the colonies. It occupies Oude Delft 89–95 on the old canal. Architecture is only a minor programme here, taught by J.E. ter Winkel: construction comes first, design is an afterthought.

1842年，荷蘭國王威廉二世（Willem II）在台夫特設立皇家學院（Koninklijke Akademie），培養土木工程師與派赴殖民地的技術官僚，校舍位於老運河Oude Delft 89–95號。建築在此只是一門輔系學程，由ter Winkel講授：構造優先，設計只是附帶。

Oude Delft 95
02
No. 02 · 1864–1905

Architecture gets its own professor建築系有了自己的教授

The Academy becomes a Polytechnic School and architecture a course of its own. Eugen Gugel is appointed the first professor of 'schoone bouwkunst' (fine architecture) and teaches for 38 years, publishing the four-volume Architectonische Vormleer (1880–88). In 1905 the school becomes the Technische Hoogeschool, and architecture a five-year department. The first three women had enrolled in 1904.

學院改制為綜合技術學校（Polytechnische School），建築首次成為獨立的修業類別。德籍建築師古格爾（Eugen Gugel）成為首位「美的建築」教授，一教三十八年，並出版四卷本《建築形式學》（Architectonische Vormleer，1880–1888）。1905年升格為技術高等學院，建築系成為五年制的獨立學系；前一年，首批三位女性已註冊入學。

Oude Delft 89–95
03
No. 03 · 1905

Berlage speaks in Delft貝拉赫在台夫特的一場演講

H.P. Berlage, the father of modern Dutch architecture, never belonged to the Delft School and built his career in Amsterdam. In 1905, though, the Delft student society Practische Studie invited him to lecture on 'the probable development of architecture': concrete as the material of the future, less ornament, beauty in construction. The student who reported on it was Granpré Molière, in his first published text on architecture.

被譽為荷蘭現代建築之父的貝拉赫（H.P. Berlage）並不屬於台夫特學派，他的主要舞台在阿姆斯特丹。然而1905年，台夫特學生社團Practische Studie邀他演講〈論建築可能的發展〉，談混凝土作為未來的建材、裝飾的減省與構造之美。為學生週報報導這場演講的，正是當時仍是學生的格蘭普雷・莫利耶，那是他第一篇公開發表的建築文字。

「Delftse School不是貝拉赫的門派，而是在貝拉赫提出的問題上，走向了另一條更保守的路。」
“The Delft School was not Berlage's school; it took the questions he raised down a more conservative road.”
Delft, 1905
04
No. 04 · 1924–1953

The professor the students chose學生推舉的教授

On the students' own proposal, Marinus Jan Granpré Molière is appointed professor. Co-designer of the Vreewijk garden village in Rotterdam (1916–19), he teaches an architecture rooted in tradition, craft and brick. He saw himself building on Cuypers and Berlage, but prized the 'unconscious' beauty Berlage rejected. Until 1953 his Delft School dominates design teaching and keeps the modernist Nieuwe Bouwen out of the curriculum.

在學生提議下，格蘭普雷・莫利耶（M.J. Granpré Molière）獲聘為教授。他參與設計鹿特丹的Vreewijk花園村（1916–1919），主張建築根植於地方傳統、手工與磚造。他自認承接居伊柏斯與貝拉赫，卻更著迷於貝拉赫所拒斥的「無意識之美」。直到1953年，「台夫特學派」主導設計教學，把現代主義的「新建築」擋在課程之外。

Vreewijk, Rotterdam
05
No. 05 · 1933–1945

A building of its own第一座自己的系館

Architecture moves into its own building at Oude Delft 39, converted by A.J. van der Steur. Outside, modernism is growing: Jan Duiker graduated as a modernist in 1927, and students ran their own international course on new architecture in 1930. Under the German occupation, professor R.L.A. Schoemaker, leader of a resistance group, is executed in 1942.

1933年，建築系遷入Oude Delft 39號，由范德史圖爾（A.J. van der Steur）改建。校外的現代主義正在成長：杜伊克（Jan Duiker）1927年以現代主義者之姿畢業，1930年學生自辦新建築國際課程。德國佔領期間，領導抵抗組織的舒馬克教授（R.L.A. Schoemaker）於1942年遭處決。

Oude Delft 39
06
No. 06 · 1947

A De Stijl member teaches urbanism風格派成員來教都市計畫

Nine new professors arrive, among them Jo van den Broek (architecture, to 1964) and Cornelis van Eesteren (urbanism, to 1967). Van Eesteren had joined De Stijl in 1923, chaired CIAM from 1930 to 1947 and drew Amsterdam's General Extension Plan (AUP, 1934) with Th.K. van Lohuizen. Its western garden cities, Slotermeer among them, were built after the war.

戰後台夫特一口氣聘任九位新教授，包括范登布魯克（Jo van den Broek，任教至1964年）與范埃斯特倫（Cornelis van Eesteren，任教至1967年）。後者1923年加入風格派（De Stijl）、1930至1947年主持CIAM，並與范洛赫森擬定1934年的《阿姆斯特丹擴展計畫》（AUP）；Slotermeer等西部花園城市即在戰後依此興建。

「一位風格派成員入主台夫特都市計畫講座，幾乎宣告了Delftse School一統天下的結束。」
“A De Stijl member in the chair of urbanism all but ended the Delft School's monopoly.”
Slotermeer, Amsterdam
07
No. 07 · 1957–1966

Modernism builds the campus現代主義蓋出校園

A 1957 curriculum reform introduces Vormstudie, modelled on the Bauhaus preliminary course. Jaap Bakema becomes professor in 1963 and teaches until his death in 1981. His firm with Van den Broek designs the campus Aula (1959–66), a concrete auditorium that still anchors the TU district, and the new architecture building.

1957年的課程改革引入以包浩斯預備課程為樣本的「形態研究」（Vormstudie）。貝克瑪（Jaap Bakema）1963年起任教直到1981年過世；他與范登布魯克的事務所設計了校園大禮堂（Aula，1959–1966）與新建築系館，這座清水混凝土禮堂至今仍是TU校區的地標。

Aula, Mekelweg 5
08
No. 08 · 1969–1973

One person, one vote一人一票的學系

In May 1969 the faculty's general assembly decides that staff and students will govern the department together, on the basis of universal suffrage. A year later architecture moves into the new building on Berlageweg, the street named after Berlage, first drawn by Van den Broek & Bakema for a 1956 competition. Students publish the manifesto De elite; in 1973 the University Governance Reform Act (WUB) reorganises the department into thirteen sections.

1969年5月，全系大會決議以普選為基礎，師生共同治理系所。隔年，建築系搬進以貝拉赫命名的Berlageweg上的新系館，其設計可追溯至范登布魯克與貝克瑪1956年的競圖。學生會Stylos發表宣言《菁英》（De elite）；1973年《大學治理改革法》（WUB）上路，學系重組為十三個學科組。

Berlageweg 1
09
No. 09 · 1970–1999

Buildings that users complete由使用者完成的建築

Herman Hertzberger teaches at Delft from 1970 to 1999. With Aldo van Eyck and Bakema he shapes Dutch Structuralism: architecture as a framework that users fill in and adapt. His Centraal Beheer offices in Apeldoorn (1968–72) are the movement's test bed. In 1979 Franziska Bollerey becomes the faculty's first female professor; in 1986 the school is renamed TU Delft.

赫茲伯格（Herman Hertzberger）1970至1999年在台夫特任教，與凡艾克、貝克瑪共同推動荷蘭結構主義：建築是一套讓使用者填滿、改造的框架，而非完成式的作品。他在阿佩多恩設計的Centraal Beheer辦公大樓（1968–1972）正是其實驗場。1979年博勒瑞（Franziska Bollerey）成為首位女性教授；1986年學校改名為台夫特理工大學。

Centraal Beheer, Apeldoorn
10
No. 10 · 13 May 2008

The day the building burned系館燒毀的那一天

A small fire in a coffee machine spreads through the Berlageweg building. Everyone gets out unhurt, but after 38 years of use the building, with its models, drawings and archives, is largely destroyed. The faculty's chair collection narrowly escapes and is now on permanent display.

2008年5月13日，一台咖啡機引發的小火延燒整棟Berlageweg系館。師生全數平安撤離，但這棟使用了三十八年的大樓，連同模型、圖面與檔案幾乎付之一炬；學院的椅子收藏僥倖逃過一劫，如今常設展示於新館。

Berlageweg 1
11
No. 11 · 2008–2009

A stopgap becomes a city從避難所到BK City

Within two weeks the university cancels the sale of its former main building to developer Fortis: the 'Red Chemistry' building by Gerard van Drecht (1918–23). Five firms (Fokkema, MVRDV, Kossmann.dejong, Braaksma & Roos, Octatube) design while building: ceilings come down, six-metre storeys return, glass conservatories cover two courtyards and an inner street ties it together. 32,000 m² grows to 36,000 m².

火災後兩週內，校方解除了老校舍出售給開發商Fortis的合約：這棟由范德雷赫特（Gerard van Drecht）設計、1918至1923年興建的「紅色化學館」。五家事務所邊設計邊施工，拆除吊頂、還原六公尺樓高，以玻璃溫室加蓋兩座中庭，再以一條「內街」串起全館；面積由32,000平方公尺擴充為36,000平方公尺（約10,890坪）。

12
No. 12 · 2012–2026

Here to stay從此長住

In 2012 the Berlage, the post-master school Hertzberger founded in Amsterdam in 1990 and named after H.P. Berlage, moves into the faculty. In April 2024 the university drops its plan for a new building: the faculty stays at Julianalaan for the long term. With about 2,900 students it is TU Delft's largest faculty, ranked second in the world in the 2020 QS subject ranking. On 1 January 2026 the Hortus Botanicus joined as a living lab.

2012年，赫茲伯格1990年在阿姆斯特丹創立、以貝拉赫命名的Berlage研究院遷入學院，成為碩士後學程。2024年4月，校方撤回另建新系館的計畫，建築學院將長期留在Julianalaan。如今學院約有2,900名學生，是全校最大的學院，並在2020年QS學科排名中位居世界第二；2026年1月1日，台夫特植物園也併入學院，成為「活的實驗室」。

「建築提供結構，使用者完成它。」
“The building gives the structure; its users complete it.”
