Ten architects · 十位建築師 · 1864–2025

Ten architects of Bouwkunde台夫特建築系的十位建築師

While tracing the history of the Delft architecture department, I noticed that almost every turning point coincides with the entrance of an architect: the first professor of architecture; a questioner who never taught here yet is quoted again and again; the leader of the school that kept modernism out; the modernist who walked out of its own door; the post-war rebuilders; the Structuralists; and finally the large-scale architect the faculty still debates in public. The ten profiles below run in chronological order; dates of lives and works follow the primary and public sources listed with each profile.

在整理台夫特建築系的歷史時，筆者發現這個學系的轉折，幾乎都對應著幾位建築師的出場：第一位建築教授、從未在此任教卻反覆被引用的提問者、把現代主義擋在門外的學派領袖、從門內走出的現代主義者、戰後的重建者、結構主義者，以及最後一位與學院公開辯論的大尺度建築師。以下十篇簡介依時序排列，作品年代與生平以文末所列一手與公開資料為準。

01
1832–1905 · First professor of architecture at Delft, 1864–1902

Eugen Gugel古格爾｜台夫特第一位建築教授（1864–1902）

把建築從工程的附屬，教成一門獨立的學問

Teaching architecture as a discipline of its own, not an appendix to engineering

古格爾（Eugen Gugel）1832年生於普法爾茨的貝格察伯恩（Bergzabern），1850至1856年在慕尼黑的綜合技術學校與藝術學院受訓，師事建築師朗格（Ludwig Lange）。他早年任職巴伐利亞國鐵，參與布倫納鐵路（Brenner）的工程，也替下巴伐利亞設計了數座法院，甚至主持過國王馬克西米利安二世在施塔恩貝格湖畔的夏宮工程，可惜國王1864年驟逝，工程只完成了基礎。

同一年，荷蘭部長托爾貝克（Thorbecke）向朗格請益，朗格推薦了古格爾；1864年8月17日，一紙皇家敕令讓他成為台夫特綜合技術學校的建築教授，此後一教就是三十八年。他出版的四卷本《建築形式學》（Architectonische Vormleer，1880–1888）與《建築風格史》，是荷蘭第一代建築工程師的共同教科書。

筆者認為，古格爾的意義不在於某一棟建築，而在於他把「美的建築」（schoone bouwkunst）帶進一所以工程為主的學校，讓建築在台夫特第一次擁有自己的教授、自己的課表與自己的教科書。日後所有關於傳統與現代的爭論，恐怕都是在他打下的這個制度基礎上展開的。

Eugen Gugel was born in 1832 in Bergzabern in the Palatinate and trained from 1850 to 1856 at the Polytechnic School and the Academy of Fine Arts in Munich under the architect Ludwig Lange. He started out with the Bavarian state railways, working on structures for the Brenner line, designed several courthouses in Lower Bavaria, and even led the construction of King Maximilian II's summer palace on Lake Starnberg, until the king's sudden death in 1864 halted the work at its foundations.

That same year the Dutch minister Thorbecke asked Lange for advice, and Lange recommended Gugel. A royal decree of 17 August 1864 made him professor of architecture at the Polytechnic School in Delft, a post he held for 38 years. His four-volume Architectonische Vormleer (1880–88) and his history of building styles became the shared textbooks of the first generation of Dutch architect-engineers.

In my view, Gugel matters less for any single building than for bringing "schoone bouwkunst", fine architecture, into a school built around engineering. For the first time, architecture in Delft had its own professor, its own curriculum and its own textbooks. Every later debate between tradition and modernity was, I would argue, fought on the institutional ground he laid.

02
1856–1934 · Father of modern Dutch architecture; lectured in Delft in 1905

Hendrik Petrus Berlage貝拉赫｜荷蘭現代建築之父；1905年在台夫特演講

一棟交易所，替荷蘭現代建築定下了磚牆與空間的文法

One exchange building that set the grammar of brick and space for modern Dutch architecture

貝拉赫（H.P. Berlage）1856年生於阿姆斯特丹，1875至1878年就讀蘇黎世聯邦理工學院（ETH Zurich），之後在歐洲遊歷約三年。他最知名的作品是阿姆斯特丹的商品交易所（Beurs van Berlage，1898–1903）：承重的清水磚牆、毫不掩飾的構造，以及以空間為優先的設計原則，後來被視為荷蘭「即物主義」（Hollandse Zakelijkheid）的起點。

他的影響力橫跨幾乎所有1920年代的荷蘭建築流派：傳統主義者、阿姆斯特丹學派、風格派與新即物主義者，都能在他身上找到源頭。1911年訪美之後，他也成為萊特（Frank Lloyd Wright）在歐洲的重要引介者；1932年，他獲頒英國皇家建築師學會金質獎章（RIBA Royal Gold Medal）。

貝拉赫並未在台夫特任教，卻與台夫特有幾段關鍵的交會：1905年他應學生社團Practische Studie之邀演講，當時還是學生的格蘭普雷・莫利耶寫下了報導；1970年的建築系館位於以他命名的Berlageweg；2012年，以他命名的Berlage研究院遷入台夫特。換句話說，他不屬於任何一個學派，卻是每一個學派都必須回應的提問者。

H.P. Berlage was born in Amsterdam in 1856, studied at ETH Zurich from 1875 to 1878 and then travelled through Europe for about three years. His best-known work is the Amsterdam Commodity Exchange, the Beurs van Berlage (1898–1903): load-bearing bare brick, construction left visible and space put first, principles later seen as the starting point of Dutch "Zakelijkheid".

His influence runs through almost every Dutch movement of the 1920s: the traditionalists, the Amsterdam School, De Stijl and the New Objectivists all trace a line back to him. After a visit to the United States in 1911 he became an important advocate of Frank Lloyd Wright in Europe, and in 1932 he received the RIBA Royal Gold Medal.

Berlage never taught in Delft, yet his path crossed it at key moments: in 1905 he lectured for the student society Practische Studie, and the student who reported on it was Granpré Molière; the 1970 faculty building stood on Berlageweg, named after him; and in 2012 the Berlage, the school named after him, moved to Delft. He belonged to no school, yet every school had to answer his questions.

03
1883–1972 · Professor at Delft 1924–1953; founder of the Delft School

Marinus Jan Granpré Molière格蘭普雷・莫利耶｜台夫特教授（1924–1953）；台夫特學派創始者

學生推舉的教授，用傳統把現代主義擋在門外

The professor the students chose, who kept modernism out with tradition

格蘭普雷・莫利耶（M.J. Granpré Molière）1883年生於奧登博斯（Oudenbosch）。1924年，他在學生提議下獲聘為台夫特教授，直到1953年，期間主導的設計教學被稱為「台夫特學派」（Delftse School），也被視為荷蘭傳統主義建築的源頭。

他的設計從鹿特丹的Vreewijk花園村（1916–1919，與Verhagen、Kok合作）起步，強調地方傳統、手工與磚造；1927年起參與威靈厄梅爾（Wieringermeer）、1937年起參與東北圩田（Noordoostpolder）等新生地的規劃，把「村落」的理想帶進國家級的填海工程。依據Bruins（2020）的研究，他自認是承接居伊柏斯與貝拉赫，卻更著迷於自發生成的「無意識之美」。

筆者認為，他的歷史評價恐怕不能只以「反現代」一筆帶過。台夫特學派的力量，不在於它拒絕了什麼，而在於它讓一整個世代的工程師相信：建築必須回應土地、社群與生活方式。只是這份信念，後來也成了學系改革時首先被挑戰的權威。

M.J. Granpré Molière was born in Oudenbosch in 1883. In 1924, on the students' own proposal, he was appointed professor in Delft, and until 1953 his design teaching, later called the Delft School (Delftse School), dominated the department; it is also seen as the origin of Dutch traditionalist architecture.

His work began with the Vreewijk garden village in Rotterdam (1916–19, with Verhagen and Kok), stressing local tradition, craft and brick. From 1927 he worked on the planning of the Wieringermeer and from 1937 on the Noordoostpolder, carrying the ideal of the village into national land-reclamation projects. According to Bruins (2020), he saw himself as building on Cuypers and Berlage, yet was more drawn to spontaneous, "unconscious" beauty.

I would argue his legacy cannot be dismissed simply as "anti-modern". The strength of the Delft School lay not in what it rejected, but in convincing a generation of engineers that architecture must answer to land, community and ways of life. That conviction, however, also became the first authority the department's later reforms set out to challenge.

04
1890–1935 · Delft-trained pioneer of Nieuwe Bouwen

Jan Duiker杜伊克｜台夫特出身的「新建築」先驅

在傳統主義的學系裡，長出最激進的現代主義者

The most radical modernist, grown inside a traditionalist department

杜伊克（Jan Duiker）1890年生於海牙，先當了一年木工學徒，1908至1913年在台夫特修讀建築，與同學貝伊福特（Bernard Bijvoet）結為終身夥伴，1916年在海牙合開事務所。他被視為荷蘭「新建築」（Nieuwe Bouwen）最重要的建築師：讓鋼、鋼筋混凝土與玻璃以最纖細的方式呈現自身，不加裝飾，追求他所說的「精神的經濟」。

他的作品幾乎都與健康有關。希爾弗瑟姆的Zonnestraal療養院（1928，與Bijvoet合作）是為結核病患設計的康復村，依維基百科記載，正是貝拉赫推薦了這對建築師；阿姆斯特丹的露天學校（1929–1930）則讓教室向陽光與空氣打開。1932年起，他擔任刊物《de 8 en Opbouw》總編輯，主張建築應當務實，卻也必須有人性。1935年，他在Gooiland飯店的設計過程中辭世。

弔詭的是，台夫特學派最堅定的反對者，正是台夫特自己培養出來的學生。杜伊克的存在提醒我們，一個學系的思想從來不會只有一種聲音。

Jan Duiker was born in The Hague in 1890, spent a year as a carpenter's apprentice and studied architecture in Delft from 1908 to 1913, where he met his lifelong partner Bernard Bijvoet; in 1916 they opened an office together in The Hague. He is regarded as the most important architect of Dutch Nieuwe Bouwen: steel, reinforced concrete and glass shown as slender as possible, without ornament, in pursuit of what he called "spiritual economy".

Almost all his work concerns health. The Zonnestraal sanatorium in Hilversum (1928, with Bijvoet) was a recovery colony for tuberculosis patients, and according to Wikipedia it was Berlage who recommended the pair; the Open Air School in Amsterdam (1929–30) opened its classrooms to sun and air. From 1932 he edited the journal de 8 en Opbouw, arguing that architecture should be businesslike yet humane. He died in 1935 while still working on the Gooiland hotel.

The irony is that the Delft School's most determined opponent was trained in Delft itself. Duiker reminds us that a department never speaks with a single voice.

05
1898–1978 · Professor of architecture at Delft 1947–1964

Jo van den Broek范登布魯克｜台夫特建築教授（1947–1964）

把建築師帶進戰後住宅生產的現代主義者

The modernist who brought architects into post-war housing production

范登布魯克（Jo van den Broek）1924年在台夫特完成建築學業，1927年開業，是荷蘭新建築運動的創始成員之一。1937年起，他與布林克曼（Johannes Brinkman）合組事務所，二戰後投入鹿特丹遭轟炸後的重建規劃；1951年，事務所更名為「范登布魯克與貝克瑪」（Van den Broek en Bakema）。

1947年，他成為台夫特戰後首批新聘教授之一，任教至1964年，並擔任學院的管理職務。他主張把設計與研究分開來談，也積極推動建築師的專業化，並參與創立鹿特丹的建築研究中心Bouwcentrum。他與貝克瑪合作的鹿特丹Lijnbaan商店街（1949–1953），是戰後鹿特丹重建最具代表性的步行商業街。

筆者認為，范登布魯克的意義在於讓建築師的角色從「藝術家」轉為「社會生產的一環」：當國家需要大量住宅時，建築教育也必須回答如何大量、快速又有品質地蓋好房子。

Jo van den Broek completed his architecture studies in Delft in 1924, started practising in 1927 and was one of the founders of the Dutch Nieuwe Bouwen. From 1937 he worked with Johannes Brinkman, and after the war the firm took part in planning the reconstruction of bombed Rotterdam; in 1951 it became Van den Broek en Bakema.

In 1947 he was among the first professors Delft appointed after the war, teaching until 1964 and holding management roles in the faculty. He insisted on keeping design and research distinct, pushed for the professionalisation of architects and co-founded Bouwcentrum in Rotterdam. The Lijnbaan shopping street in Rotterdam (1949–53), designed with Bakema, is the most iconic pedestrian street of Rotterdam's post-war reconstruction.

In my view, Van den Broek shifted the architect's role from "artist" to part of social production: when a country needs housing on a massive scale, architectural education has to answer how to build fast, at scale and with quality.

06
1914–1981 · Professor at Delft 1963–1981; Team 10

Jaap Bakema貝克瑪｜台夫特教授（1963–1981）；Team 10核心成員

把城市當成一連串可以被使用的關係來設計

Designing the city as a chain of relationships people can use

貝克瑪（Jaap Bakema）1914年生於格羅寧根，先後在格羅寧根高等技術學校與阿姆斯特丹建築學院學習，師事斯塔姆（Mart Stam），1941年以優異成績畢業。1946年起參加國際現代建築會議（CIAM），1955年擔任秘書，也是質疑功能主義、主張回到人本的Team 10核心成員，並與凡艾克、赫茲伯格共同編輯《Forum》雜誌。

他與范登布魯克合作的作品遍及荷蘭與德國：鹿特丹Lijnbaan商店街、柏林Interbau國際建築展的漢莎區住宅（Hansaviertel，1957）、德國馬爾市民中心（1958–1962）、特爾訥曾市政廳（1963–1972），以及台夫特的建築系館與大禮堂。1963年起他在台夫特任教，直到1981年過世；1965年起也兼任漢堡的教授。

貝克瑪最打動筆者的，是他對「建築師社會責任」的堅持。他設計的不只是建築物，而是建築物之間的街道、廣場與相遇；這也是今天台灣討論步行街區時，仍然值得回頭閱讀Lijnbaan的原因。

Jaap Bakema was born in Groningen in 1914 and studied at the Groningen Higher Technical School and then at the Amsterdam Academy of Architecture under Mart Stam, graduating with distinction in 1941. From 1946 he attended CIAM, becoming its secretary in 1955; he was a core member of Team 10, which questioned functionalism and called for a return to humanism, and co-edited the magazine Forum with Van Eyck and Hertzberger.

His work with Van den Broek spans the Netherlands and Germany: the Lijnbaan in Rotterdam, housing in the Hansaviertel for the Interbau exhibition in Berlin (1957), the civic centre of Marl (1958–62), the town hall of Terneuzen (1963–72), and the faculty building and Aula in Delft. He taught in Delft from 1963 until his death in 1981, and from 1965 also in Hamburg.

What moves me most is his insistence on the architect's social responsibility. He designed not just buildings, but the streets, squares and encounters between them, which is why the Lijnbaan is still worth revisiting whenever Taiwan debates pedestrian districts.

07
1897–1988 · Professor of urbanism at Delft 1947–1967; CIAM chair 1930–1947

Cornelis van Eesteren范埃斯特倫｜台夫特都市計畫教授（1947–1967）；CIAM主席（1930–1947）

從風格派的構成圖，走到一整座城市的擴展計畫

From a De Stijl composition to the extension plan of an entire city

范埃斯特倫（Cornelis van Eesteren）1897年生於阿爾布拉瑟丹（Alblasserdam）。他早年與杜斯伯格（Theo van Doesburg）合作，1923年加入風格派（De Stijl），兩人共同製作的建築模型曾在巴黎展出；之後他贏得柏林菩提樹下大街（Unter den Linden）的設計競圖，並到威瑪的國立建築高等學校擔任客座教授。

1929至1959年間，他任職於阿姆斯特丹市政府都市計畫部門，與范洛赫森（Th.K. van Lohuizen）以人口與土地需求的調查為基礎，擬定1934年的《阿姆斯特丹擴展計畫》（AUP），戰後的西部花園城市即依此興建。1930至1947年，他擔任國際現代建築會議（CIAM）主席；戰後則成為台夫特的都市計畫教授，也參與南部須德海圩田與萊利斯塔德（Lelystad）的規劃。

筆者特別喜歡他的這條軌跡：一位畫過紅黃藍構成圖的風格派成員，最後畫的是一整座城市的土地使用圖。這也正是本故事地圖採用風格派視覺語言的原因。

Cornelis van Eesteren was born in Alblasserdam in 1897. Early on he worked with Theo van Doesburg and joined De Stijl in 1923; their architectural models were exhibited in Paris. He then won the design competition for Unter den Linden in Berlin and became a visiting professor at the State School of Building in Weimar.

From 1929 to 1959 he worked in Amsterdam's town planning department, where, with Th.K. van Lohuizen and on the basis of surveys of population and land needs, he drew up the 1934 General Extension Plan (AUP); the western garden cities were built to it after the war. He chaired CIAM from 1930 to 1947, became professor of urban planning in Delft after the war, and worked on the plans for the southern IJsselmeer polders and Lelystad.

I am especially fond of this trajectory: a De Stijl member who once drew red, yellow and blue compositions ended up drawing the land-use map of an entire city. It is also why this story map speaks the visual language of De Stijl.

1918–1999 · Professor at Delft 1966–1984; Team 10 co-founder

Aldo van Eyck凡艾克｜台夫特教授（1966–1984）；Team 10共同創立者

用孤兒院證明，建築可以像一座小城市

An orphanage that proved a building can be like a small city

凡艾克（Aldo van Eyck）1918年生於德里伯亨（Driebergen），在英國與海牙完成中學，1942年畢業於蘇黎世聯邦理工學院。他是CIAM成員，1954年共同創立Team 10，主張拒絕僵化的功能主義、回到人本；1959至1963年間與貝克瑪、赫茲伯格共同編輯《Forum》雜誌，是荷蘭結構主義最具影響力的人物之一。

他的代表作阿姆斯特丹市立孤兒院（Burgerweeshuis，1955–1960），以重複的方形單元與圓頂組成「像城市一樣的房子」；他也設計了東北圩田的新村Nagele（1948–1954）、Slotermeer的長者住宅（1951–1952）與阿姆斯特丹的Hubertushuis（1973–1978）。他先後任教於阿姆斯特丹建築學院（1954–1959）與台夫特（1966–1984）。

對照本故事地圖，凡艾克在Slotermeer蓋過長者住宅，正好把范埃斯特倫的城市計畫與貝克瑪、赫茲伯格的結構主義串在一起。台夫特的這幾位教授，其實是在同一片土地上彼此對話。

Aldo van Eyck was born in Driebergen in 1918, went to school in England and The Hague, and graduated from ETH Zurich in 1942. A member of CIAM, he co-founded Team 10 in 1954, rejecting rigid functionalism in favour of humanism, and from 1959 to 1963 co-edited Forum with Bakema and Hertzberger; he is one of the most influential figures of Dutch Structuralism.

His best-known work, the Amsterdam Municipal Orphanage (Burgerweeshuis, 1955–60), assembles repeated square units and domes into "a house like a city". He also designed the new village of Nagele in the Noordoostpolder (1948–54), housing for the elderly in Slotermeer (1951–52) and the Hubertushuis in Amsterdam (1973–78). He taught at the Amsterdam Academy of Architecture (1954–59) and in Delft (1966–84).

Read alongside this story map, Van Eyck's housing for the elderly in Slotermeer ties Van Eesteren's city plan to the Structuralism of Bakema and Hertzberger. These Delft professors were, in fact, in conversation on the same ground.

1932– · Professor at Delft 1970–1999; founder of the Berlage Institute

Herman Hertzberger赫茲伯格｜台夫特教授（1970–1999）；Berlage研究院創辦人

建築提供結構，使用者完成它

The building gives the structure; its users complete it

赫茲伯格（Herman Hertzberger）1932年生於阿姆斯特丹，1958年畢業於台夫特，1959至1963年與凡艾克、貝克瑪共同編輯《Forum》雜誌，1970至1999年在台夫特任教，1990年在阿姆斯特丹創立以貝拉赫命名的Berlage研究院。2012年，他獲頒英國皇家建築師學會金質獎章。

他的代表作阿佩多恩Centraal Beheer保險公司辦公大樓（1968–1972），以大量重複的方形單元組成，讓員工自己布置、填滿空間；台夫特的蒙特梭利學校（1966–1970）與實驗性的Diagoon住宅（1971）也都在台夫特。他寫的《Lessons for Students in Architecture》（1991）至今仍是許多建築系學生的入門讀物。

這本故事地圖反覆出現的一句話，「建築提供結構，使用者完成它」，正是從赫茲伯格的思想延伸而來。BK City的師生在十六年間把一棟避難用的老校舍「使用」成自己的家，大概是對這句話最好的實證。

Herman Hertzberger was born in Amsterdam in 1932 and graduated from Delft in 1958. From 1959 to 1963 he co-edited Forum with Van Eyck and Bakema; he taught in Delft from 1970 to 1999 and in 1990 founded the Berlage Institute in Amsterdam, named after H.P. Berlage. In 2012 he received the RIBA Royal Gold Medal.

His best-known work, the Centraal Beheer insurance offices in Apeldoorn (1968–72), is built from many repeated square units that staff furnish and fill themselves. The Montessori School (1966–70) and the experimental Diagoon houses (1971) are both in Delft, and his Lessons for Students in Architecture (1991) is still a first book for many students.

The line that recurs throughout this story map, "the building gives the structure; its users complete it", grows out of Hertzberger's thinking. The way BK City's staff and students turned an emergency shelter into their home over sixteen years is perhaps its best proof.

1937–2025 · Professor at Delft 1970–2003

Carel Weeber韋伯｜台夫特教授（1970–2003）

對「小而美」說不的大尺度建築師

The large-scale architect who said no to "small is beautiful"

韋伯（Carel Weeber）1937年生於奈梅亨（Nijmegen），在台夫特修讀建築，1966年以阿姆斯特丹中央車站的新設計案獲得羅馬大獎（Prix de Rome），1970至2003年擔任台夫特教授，1993至1998年出任荷蘭建築師協會（BNA）主席。

1970年代末，荷蘭住宅設計流行小尺度、溫馨的「新拘謹」（Nieuwe truttigheid），韋伯則以一系列大尺度的作品回應，其中最著名的是鹿特丹南區的De Peperklip集合住宅（1979–1982），以一道迴紋針形狀的長條量體圍出整個街廓。1997年，他提出「狂野居住」（Het Wilde Wonen），批評荷蘭住宅建設過於僵化，主張讓居民有更多自由。2025年2月，他在比利時魯汶辭世。

同年9月16日，BK Talks以「Carel Weeber and our contemporary challenges」為題在BK City東側溫室舉辦講座，筆者也在現場。一位建築師過世半年後，學院用一場公開講座追問他的提問在今天是否仍然成立，這種與前輩辯論而非膜拜的態度，恐怕正是台夫特最值得借鏡之處。

Carel Weeber was born in Nijmegen in 1937 and studied architecture in Delft. In 1966 he won the Prix de Rome with a design for a new Amsterdam Central Station; he was a professor in Delft from 1970 to 2003 and chaired the Association of Dutch Architects (BNA) from 1993 to 1998.

In the late 1970s, when Dutch housing favoured the small, cosy scale of "Nieuwe truttigheid", Weeber answered with a series of large-scale works, most famously De Peperklip in Rotterdam-Zuid (1979–82), a single paper-clip-shaped block enclosing an entire urban block. In 1997 he coined "Het Wilde Wonen" (Wild Living), criticising the rigidity of Dutch housing and calling for more freedom for residents. He died in Leuven, Belgium, in February 2025.

On 16 September that year, BK Talks held "Carel Weeber and our contemporary challenges" in BK City's east conservatory, and I was there. Half a year after an architect's death, the faculty asked in a public lecture whether his questions still stand today. Debating one's predecessors rather than revering them is, I think, what Delft offers most to learn from.
